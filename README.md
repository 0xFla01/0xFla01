## Projects

**[rl-agent-2d-rpg](https://github.com/0xFla01/rl-agent-2d-rpg)**
My bachelor's thesis. A PPO agent learns to play a 2D action RPG I built in
Godot 4. Behavioural cloning from my own gameplay, curriculum learning, a lot
of reward shaping, and a comparison against Claude playing zero shot.
`Python` `stable-baselines3` `Godot 4` `GDScript`

**[spot](https://github.com/0xFla01/spot)**
A full stack Solana platform built around its own token, and a social
experiment in greed, ownership and scarcity. Every spot on the page is for
sale. Anyone can take yours by paying 1.5x what you paid, so prices grow
exponentially with every flip, and a cut of every trade is burned on chain
forever. The more people fight over the wall, the less token supply is left.
Under the hood: single signature transactions that pay the previous owner,
burn tokens and log the purchase all at once, server side verification of
every trade on chain, Phantom wallet integration, an image upload studio and
a live wall that stays in sync for every visitor.
`JavaScript` `Vite` `Node` `Solana` `web3.js` `SPL Token`
